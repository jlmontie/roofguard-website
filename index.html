02 — The Problem

Most Utah Roofs Fail Earlier Than They Should.

  1. 01

    UV Degradation

    High-altitude sun dries out shingles and breaks down the asphalt binders that hold them together.

  2. 02

    Granule Loss

    Each freeze/thaw cycle accelerates granule loss, exposing the shingle base to faster wear.

  3. 03

    Cracking & Leaks

    Curling and cracked shingles let water in. Once leaks start, the timeline to replacement gets shorter fast.

We slow that process down.

06 — FAQ

Common
Questions.

If your question isn't here, call us. Direct answers, no pressure.

Will this void my shingle manufacturer's warranty?

For most asphalt shingle warranties on roofs past their initial coverage period, RoofGuard does not affect coverage. We review your specific shingle and warranty status during the free assessment and tell you straight.

How long does the coating actually last?

In Utah's climate, expect 5–10 years of added roof life depending on roof age, slope, sun exposure, and shingle condition at time of application. We'll give you a realistic range for your specific roof.

What if my roof is already leaking?

RoofGuard isn't a leak repair product. If your roof is actively leaking or has structural damage, you likely need targeted repair or replacement instead. We'll tell you that during the assessment.

Is this just paint?

No. It's a protective coating engineered to bond to asphalt shingles, block UV, and slow granule loss. Paint sits on top and peels. RoofGuard cures into the shingle surface.

How does it hold up under Utah snow?

The coating is designed for the Mountain West climate — heavy snow, ice, and wide temperature swings. It flexes with the shingle through freeze/thaw without cracking.
