🧩 VISUAL STYLE RULES

Do:

* Use strong contrast
* Keep layouts simple
* Use real roof images

Don’t:

* Overuse icons
* Add gradients everywhere
* Use generic stock imagery of “happy families”

⸻

🧠 COPY PRINCIPLES (VERY IMPORTANT)

Lead with:

* Cost avoided
* Time extended
* Practical benefit

Avoid:

* Overpromising
* Technical overload
* “Revolutionary” language
